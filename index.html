Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Omer AI Receptionist ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our automated messaging service on WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

When you send a message to a business using our service, we may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number;
  • Your WhatsApp profile name;
  • The content of the messages you send to the business;
  • Message metadata such as timestamps and delivery status.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Provide automated responses to your inquiries on behalf of the business you contacted;
  • Route your conversation to a human representative when needed;
  • Maintain and improve the quality and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How Messages Are Processed

Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., and are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. To generate automated replies, message content may be processed by third-party artificial intelligence service providers under strict confidentiality terms. These providers process the data only to generate responses and do not use it for their own purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain conversation data only for as long as necessary to provide the service to the business you contacted, after which it is deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. You can also stop interacting with the service at any moment by simply not sending further messages.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
